CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

CMU's published usage terms (checked 2026-11-01):
This data is free for use in research projects. You may include this data
in commercially-sold products, but you may not resell this data directly,
even in converted form.

These captured motions are included as part of the colinvrr website and are
not offered for sale as motion data. The original data remains subject to
CMU's terms, including the prohibition on selling the motion data itself.

BVH conversion: Bruce Hahne, MotionBuilder-friendly 2010 release.
Conversion documentation and usage rights are retained in
tools/vendor/cmu-mocap/READMEFIRST.txt. Bruce Hahne places no additional
restrictions on the conversion.
Conversion source: https://sites.google.com/a/cgspeed.com/cgspeed/motion-capture/cmu-bvh-conversion
Download mirror: https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap

The website retains only sampled joint positions, root orientation and
head direction, extracted at 30 Hz by tools/extract-resident-mocap.cjs.
The source recording is 120 Hz. The added calibration T-pose (frame zero)
is excluded. Coordinates are root-centered horizontally, yaw-canonical,
Y-up, with source standing height normalized to 270 local units. Root
travel and yaw are retained separately. Support-foot correction and limb
length retargeting belong to the website's custom character controller.
The partner-supported deep dips in the two full salsa captures are excluded
from the selected contiguous phrases to suit this solo street character.
